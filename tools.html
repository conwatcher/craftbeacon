What happens to your work

We keep three things: your email address, a running count of the words you have submitted, and your finished report for seven days. Your manuscript itself is not stored — it is read, analyzed, and discarded when your report is written. Reports are generated using the Anthropic API, which does not train on submitted content and deletes inputs and outputs within thirty days.

You are responsible for ensuring your use of these tools complies with the laws and regulations that apply where you live and work.
